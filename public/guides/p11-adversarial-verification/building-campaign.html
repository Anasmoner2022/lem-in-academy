CHAPTER 8 OF 14

Run differential and mutation campaigns

Turn correctness claims into reproducible evidence.

Before writing code

  1. What makes two implementations independent enough to catch modelling mistakes?
  2. How will a random discrepancy be reproduced?

Build in small passes

  1. Enumerate all edge masks for small labelled simple graphs.
  2. Compare no-route outcomes and minimum turns, then replay every planner schedule.
  3. Record case counts and preserve minimal mismatches; mutate valid schedules to exercise the checker.

A grounded hint

Matching one canned example is weak evidence. A bug that allows two opposing uses of one tunnel may require an intermediate occupancy state to expose it.

Trace and self-check

For V=2..5 there are 2+8+64+1024=1098 labelled graph masks with fixed endpoints. Across n=1..3, the baseline campaign has 3294 cases before any optional V=6 samples.

Exit gate

The report records zero unexplained mismatches, all budget skips, and at least one mutation caught for every required movement invariant.
