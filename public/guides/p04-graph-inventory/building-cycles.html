CHAPTER 8 OF 14

Distinguish cycles from parent edges

Report cyclic structure without rejecting a legitimate graph.

Before writing code

  1. Why is seeing your parent again expected in an undirected graph?
  2. Can a cycle detector replace the parser's self-link checks?

Build in small passes

  1. Store parent information for iterative traversal.
  2. Detect a visited neighbor different from the parent.
  3. Test a line, a triangle and a triangle with a disconnected isolated vertex.

A grounded hint

Do not infer a cycle from E≥V across the whole graph without checking components. Structural tests should identify why the trace closes.

Trace and self-check

Trace a-b-c-a. When c observes a, a is already visited and is not c's parent. Compare a-b, where b sees only its parent.

Exit gate

The detector reports a triangle as cyclic, never spins, and preserves it as valid graph data.
