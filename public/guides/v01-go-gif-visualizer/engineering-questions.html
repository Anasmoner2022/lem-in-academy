Engineering Questions and Socratic Vision

Discuss these after the build. Use your own traces and code to defend a decision; there are no hidden answers.

  1. Why should a visualizer validate movement even when it consumes your own solver's stdout?
  2. Can a beautiful animation mislead a reviewer about minimum turns?
  3. How do you balance visual separation of coincident rooms with preserving supplied coordinates?
  4. What is the simplest accessible representation of every ant when start/end contain many?
  5. Why is multi-file output finalization more complicated than renaming one GIF?
  6. What correctness claim survives when a viewer changes playback timing?
  7. How would you extend the renderer while keeping the Go-only standard-library boundary?
