CHAPTER 9 OF 15

Verify the real pipeline and animation

Combine machine checks with a viewed replay.

Before writing code

  1. What can decode-back tests prove that visual inspection cannot?
  2. What remains viewer-dependent?

Build in small passes

  1. Run the corridor fixture through stdin and decode the resulting GIF back in Go tests.
  2. Compare frame count, delay array, dimensions and final ant positions.
  3. Inspect desktop and mobile-sized image viewing plus legend/replay readability, recording the viewer used.

A grounded hint

Some viewers ignore tiny delays; the encoded metadata remains testable. This bonus uses 10-centisecond subframes to avoid relying on extremely short timing.

Trace and self-check

For T=3, expect 4T+2=14 frames, initial/final delays 40 and twelve intermediate delays 10.

Exit gate

The GIF visibly moves ants along tunnels, has no ghost trails, ends with all ants at end and has accurate companion text.
