Practical Questions

Each task should take a few minutes against your finished artifact. Keep the resulting test or observation.

  1. Generate the corridor replay and inspect turn 2 against replay.txt.
  2. Decode the GIF and assert 14 frames.
  3. Add coincident room coordinates and document the display result.
  4. Feed an invalid-data line through stdin.
  5. Create an output collision and confirm original bytes remain unchanged.
  6. Force a low pixel budget and confirm there is no success artifact.
  7. Record the viewer and shell used for the final pipeline check.
