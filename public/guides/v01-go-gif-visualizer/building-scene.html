CHAPTER 7 OF 15

Render a faithful colony and ant motion

Keep the graphical story consistent with logical replay state.

Before writing code

  1. How will terminal crowds be shown without losing ant counts?
  2. How do coincident coordinates affect visibility but not identity?

Build in small passes

  1. Map the static scene once and render all tunnels/rooms in stable order.
  2. Choose a documented terminal/overlap display policy.
  3. Render each interpolation frame from immutable old/new positions and preserve exact boundary frames.

A grounded hint

Use the text replay to check any visually ambiguous terminal crowd. The image is a display of state, not the authoritative identity store.

Trace and self-check

Inspect a two-ant pipeline on s-a-t. At the middle boundary, one ant is at end and the other at a; a persistent trail must not suggest two ants share a.

Exit gate

First, intermediate and final keyframes agree with independently decoded logical snapshots.
