Engineering Questions and Socratic Vision

Discuss these after the build. Use your own traces and code to defend a decision; there are no hidden answers.

  1. Why is a valid EOF different from a successful completed replay?
  2. What metadata should the renderer receive so it can stay ignorant of token grammar?
  3. How can aliasing corrupt a replay without changing the original transcript?
  4. Should a visualizer accept a legal but suboptimal schedule, and how will it describe that distinction?
  5. Which failures belong to the producer, the decoder and the process harness?
  6. What portability claims can you make without testing every shell version?
  7. How does retaining immutable turn boundaries simplify later interpolation?
