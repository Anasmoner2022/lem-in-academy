Required reading

Mandatory before building

  • Princeton Algorithms: Maximum Flow — Maximum flow; minimum s-t cut; augmenting paths. Learn capacity, conservation and the optimality certificate.
  • Princeton maxflow study guide — Residual network; Ford-Fulkerson details. Understand reverse capacity and BFS over residual arcs.
  • MIT 6.854 maxflow notes — Ford-Fulkerson Algorithm; integral capacities and O(m|f|) bound. Justify termination and a bound when the target flow is capped.

Recommended while building

  • Go testing — Subtests; Benchmarks. Separate arc-pair invariants from whole-network behavior.

Reuse and readiness

Finish these builds first: P07 · Traffic and Ant Allocation Lab.

Reading is preparation. Be ready to explain the invariant you will test before implementing it.
