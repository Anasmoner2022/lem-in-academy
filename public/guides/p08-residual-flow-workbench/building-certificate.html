Certify maximum flow and limits

Distinguish target feasibility from full exhaustion.

Before writing code

  1. When is a reachable-side cut a minimum-cut certificate?
  2. Why do integer capacities guarantee progress?

Build in small passes

  1. Compute reachable residual vertices after an exhausted search.
  2. Sum original outgoing cut capacities and compare to flow value.
  3. Add overflow checks, target-zero handling and a fresh-state Solve contract.

A grounded hint

An early stop at target one on a network supporting two is a successful feasibility result, but its residual reachable set need not be a separating minimum cut.

Trace and self-check

For s→a capacity 2 and a→t capacity 1, max flow and bottleneck cut capacity are one. For target zero, no mutation should be needed.

Exit gate

Independent conservation and cut checks pass on several networks, including disconnected and zero-capacity cases.
