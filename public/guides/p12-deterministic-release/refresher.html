Focused refresher

Signed integer overflow in Go does not panic automatically. Check multiplication and addition before creating time-layer counts, capacities, buffer lengths or slices. Perform calculations in a fixed-width type and verify conversion to int on the required 64-bit target.

For T layers of transitions, estimate 2(T+1)V+2TE vertices and at most (2T+1)V+5TE original arcs; residual arcs double the latter. Limits must be checked before allocation. Also bound accumulated output and work across all binary-search probes.

Keep three outcomes distinct: invalid input, a mathematically infeasible deadline, and inability to complete a computation within a resource budget. A timeout cannot be converted into false in binary search. A writer failure may happen after bytes have reached stdout; report it and exit without pretending the transcript is complete.

Logical turn concurrency does not require goroutines. A deterministic single-threaded planner is easier to bound and reproduce.
