Overview

Build the operational boundary around the planner: checked model-size estimates, a budget policy, stable transcript formatting and repeatable release evidence. The artifact is a small release harness plus tests proving that limits and output handling do not silently change the optimization problem.

Reuse the algorithms from P10 and the checker/oracle from P11. Your new work handles overflow, process streams, cancellation and repeatability. An honest refusal is preferable to labelling an unproved heuristic answer as a minimum-turn solution.

Prerequisite gate

Finish these builds first: P11 · Adversarial Validator and Tiny Oracle.

Planning estimate: 10 focused hours before review. Add 25–50% if Go or the mathematical model is new. Learner completion remains unassessed.
