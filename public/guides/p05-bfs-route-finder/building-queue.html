CHAPTER 6 OF 14

Build the discovery queue

Create a traversal worklist with explicit visited semantics.

Before writing code

  1. Why must a vertex be marked before enqueueing?
  2. What does the queue contain after a vertex is expanded?

Build in small passes

  1. Initialize distance/seen and parent arrays for all vertices.
  2. Use a slice plus head index instead of repeated front deletion.
  3. Record queue operations in a small hand trace before implementing neighbor traversal.

A grounded hint

In a diamond s-a-t and s-b-t, t must enter the queue once even though two edges discover it.

Trace and self-check

For neighbors a then b, trace queue [s], then [s,a,b], then discovery of t from a. b must not enqueue t again.

Exit gate

A cycle and a diamond each enqueue no more than V vertices.
