Engineering Questions and Socratic Vision

Discuss these after the build. Use your own traces and code to defend a decision; there are no hidden answers.

  1. Which part of your program is trusted to decide whether a deadline is feasible, and how did you test that trust?
  2. Can your proof handle waiting, direct tunnels and ordinary cycles without special-case guesses?
  3. Why is a maximum set of disjoint routes not sufficient evidence of minimum makespan?
  4. What must change if an institution interprets receiving-room emptiness sequentially rather than as a batch?
  5. How does your extraction order determine ant IDs without changing the objective?
  6. Which resource refusals can occur for valid input, and how do you explain them honestly?
  7. Which independent evidence would convince another instructor who has not read your implementation?
  8. What assumption in your algorithm or CLI contract is academy policy rather than explicit upstream text?
