CHAPTER 7 OF 17

Integrate graph identity and route bounds

Make reachability and deterministic ordering explicit before optimization.

Before writing code

  1. Can an isolated extra room invalidate a valid start-end route?
  2. What proves the finite upper deadline?

Build in small passes

  1. Canonicalize room and edge identity using P04.
  2. Run BFS for d and reject no route deliberately.
  3. Calculate checked [d,d+n-1] bounds and retain distance metadata for proof and optional safe pruning.

A grounded hint

Coordinates are not distances. A long drawn tunnel still costs one movement turn.

Trace and self-check

Add a disconnected triangle to a valid corridor. The route and optimum remain unchanged; the parser and graph must not reject ordinary cycles.

Exit gate

Nonnumeric names, unordered definitions, cycles and disconnected extras all have acceptance tests.
