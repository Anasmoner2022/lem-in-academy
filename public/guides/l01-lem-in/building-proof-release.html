CHAPTER 11 OF 17

Prove and review the finished solver

Present evidence for legality, minimality and operational behavior.

Before writing code

  1. Which evidence proves minimum turns rather than just a valid replay?
  2. Which external audit assumptions remain institution-specific?

Build in small passes

  1. Run P11's differential campaign and targeted greedy-trap case.
  2. Retain T-1 cut or BFS-bound evidence for representative tests.
  3. Benchmark the source audit's large-ant examples and document hardware, Go version, limits and results.

A grounded hint

A slow but legal plan fails the core objective. A timeout also fails a performance gate even if it is handled gracefully; do not rename that outcome success.

Trace and self-check

For an n=1 colony, minimum equals BFS distance d. For multiple ants, show a case where capacity rather than d determines the last arrival.

Exit gate

A reviewer can reproduce tests, explain the algorithm proof and audit limits without accessing private implementation assumptions.
