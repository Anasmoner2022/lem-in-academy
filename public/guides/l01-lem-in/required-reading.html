Required reading

Mandatory before building

  • Official lem-in subject — Entire subject: Objectives, Instructions, Allowed packages, Usage, Bonus. Understand the assignment and separate mandatory behavior from bonus scope.
  • Official audit — Functional, General and Basic checks. Review movement, compatibility, error and timing expectations.
  • Official examples — All valid and invalid examples. Build fixtures from the exact source data, preserving names and coordinates.
  • Official good practices — Code; Go; Time Limitation. Review structure, naming, errors, formatting and bounded execution.

Recommended while building

  • Effective Go — Formatting; Names; Errors; Interfaces. Keep integration idiomatic and testable.
  • Go testing — Subtests; Fuzzing; Benchmarks. Produce behavior and performance evidence rather than just successful compilation.

Reuse and readiness

Finish these builds first: P12 · Deterministic and Bounded Release Lab.

Reading is preparation. Be ready to explain the invariant you will test before implementing it.
