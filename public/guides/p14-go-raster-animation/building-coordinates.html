CHAPTER 6 OF 14

Fit colony coordinates safely

Make all valid coordinate shapes visible without overflow or division by zero.

Before writing code

  1. What happens when all x coordinates are equal?
  2. Where can integer subtraction overflow before conversion?

Build in small passes

  1. Find coordinate bounds using a documented numeric representation.
  2. Compute a uniform padded scale and centered translation.
  3. Test negative, coincident, zero-span and large signed coordinates.

A grounded hint

For points (-10,0) and (10,0), the y coordinate belongs at the vertical center. A zero y span must not produce a NaN scale.

Trace and self-check

Map two coincident rooms. They share a pixel under the exact transform; a later documented display offset may separate labels, but must not change the model coordinates.

Exit gate

Every mapped point stays within padding bounds and the transform is deterministic.
