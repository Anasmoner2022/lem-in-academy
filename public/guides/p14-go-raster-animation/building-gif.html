CHAPTER 8 OF 14

Encode and inspect animation

Connect immutable keyframes to predictable GIF timing and memory use.

Before writing code

  1. How many images are stored for K turn transitions with four subframes?
  2. Which disposal choice prevents ghost trails?

Build in small passes

  1. Preflight frame count and total pixel budgets with checked arithmetic.
  2. Interpolate positions and assign explicit delays and loop behavior.
  3. Encode GIF, decode it back with gif.DecodeAll and compare metadata and endpoint frames.

A grounded hint

Do not reuse one Paletted pointer for every frame. EncodeAll would otherwise see repeated final pixels rather than the historical animation.

Trace and self-check

With two boundary snapshots, store one initial hold, four interpolated frames and one final hold: six frames. Delays are [40,10,10,10,10,40].

Exit gate

Decoded frame count, size, delays and initial/final pixel positions match the specification; animation is visually inspected.
