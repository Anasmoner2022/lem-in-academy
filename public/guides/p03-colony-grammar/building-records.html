CHAPTER 7 OF 14

Validate room and terminal records

Keep name, integer and terminal invariants independently testable.

Before writing code

  1. Why is a coordinate of -0 legal but an ant count of -0 invalid?
  2. What identifies a duplicate room when coordinates differ?

Build in small passes

  1. Validate numeric spelling before integer conversion.
  2. Create each room once and bind at most one pending marker.
  3. At EOF check count, terminal uniqueness and no pending directive.

A grounded hint

Do not use coordinates as room identity. Two valid rooms can share the same coordinate pair.

Trace and self-check

Parse rooms named hall, 9 and x-y in arbitrary order, with coincident coordinates. All three names remain distinct. An int64 overflow fails conversion.

Exit gate

Tests show duplicate name, duplicate marker, missing terminal, numeric overflow and allowed duplicate coordinates.
