Engineering Questions and Socratic Vision

Discuss these after the build. Use your own traces and code to defend a decision; there are no hidden answers.

  1. Why should a parser report the first invalid physical line instead of collecting an arbitrary map of errors?
  2. Which grammar policies are academy decisions and how would changing one affect stored fixtures?
  3. Does allowing hyphens in names make a tunnel grammar intrinsically ambiguous? Defend your resolution rule.
  4. Why should ordinary graph cycles survive parsing while a self-link does not?
  5. How can an unknown command be ignored semantically and still appear in the accepted-input echo?
  6. What property prevents a pending terminal marker from silently binding to the wrong room?
  7. Would accepting room definitions after links simplify or complicate endpoint validation?
