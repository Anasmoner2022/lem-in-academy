Full specification

Accepted language

  1. Implement Parse(lines) with phases ants, rooms, links. Preserve source line numbers. Leading/trailing horizontal whitespace may surround a data line; it remains present in Echo. Interior blank lines are invalid. Comments begin with # after trimming; exact ##start and ##end are recognized commands, other ## lines are ignored semantically.
  2. The first data line is [1-9][0-9]*, in range 1..10000. This no-sign/no-leading-zero rule is academy policy. Commands before the count may be ignored only if unknown; recognized terminal commands there are invalid.
  3. A room has exactly three whitespace-separated fields: name, x, y. The name is valid UTF-8, nonempty, contains no Unicode whitespace or controls, and begins with neither L nor #. Hyphens are allowed. Coordinates match optional sign plus decimal digits and fit a signed 64-bit Go int; use a 64-bit target. Coincident coordinates and unordered names are valid.
  4. Exactly one distinct start and one distinct end must be defined. A marker applies to the next room, skipping comments and unknown commands. Another terminal marker while one is pending, a repeated marker, a marker after links or EOF while pending is invalid.
  5. Room names cannot repeat. At the first tunnel, freeze room definitions. A tunnel has no whitespace and must have exactly one split at a hyphen whose left and right strings are known room names. Zero matches or multiple matches are invalid. Reject self-links and duplicate undirected pairs, including reversed duplicates.
  6. Reject a room after links, unknown noncommand lines, bad arity, invalid integers, overflow and missing terminals. Unknown commands are ignored even after the links start. Ordinary cycles and isolated extra rooms are permitted at this stage.
  7. Return no partial Colony on error. Store rooms by insertion order and terminals by integer index. P04 will canonicalize adjacency; Parse does not decide routes or turn count.

Limits and CLI

Apply P02's limits, plus at most 10000 rooms and 50000 links. The inspection CLI prints ants=N rooms=R links=E start=NAME end=NAME after full validation. An invalid-data error begins ERROR: invalid data format; this prep program may return it to main on stderr. L01 adopts the final stdout error policy.
