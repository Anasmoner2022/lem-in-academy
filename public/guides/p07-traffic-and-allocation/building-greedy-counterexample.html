CHAPTER 8 OF 14

Break a greedy path reservation

Separate route-set selection from allocation and motivate rerouting.

Before writing code

  1. Which vertices does s-a-b-t consume?
  2. Can two longer paths finish four ants sooner?

Build in small passes

  1. Draw the supplied nine-edge graph.
  2. Check both length-four alternatives are internally disjoint.
  3. Compute and simulate the two candidate plans, recording exactly what this comparison proves.

A grounded hint

Deleting a and b after selecting the first shortest path destroys both alternatives. Residual capacity later permits revising a choice rather than freezing it.

Trace and self-check

Shortest-only length 3 with four ants takes 6 turns. Two length-4 routes with two ants each take 5. Do not infer this from total path lengths.

Exit gate

Explain why a valid greedy schedule is not enough for a fewest-turns claim and preserve the graph for the P11 oracle.
