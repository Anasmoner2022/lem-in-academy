CHAPTER 6 OF 14

Calculate deadline capacity

Translate route length into how many ants can arrive on time.

Before writing code

  1. Why is there a +1 in T-l+1?
  2. How does a direct edge differ from unlimited terminal occupancy?

Build in small passes

  1. Write boundary tests at T=l-1, l and l+1.
  2. Sum capacities with saturation at n to avoid needless overflow.
  3. Search a safe interval from the shortest length to shortest length+n-1.

A grounded hint

A length-four path delivers its first ant at turn four, not turn three. Test that off-by-one before using the formula in a search.

Trace and self-check

For [2,4], n=4, capacities at T=3 are [2,0]; at T=4 they are [3,1].

Exit gate

Every returned deadline is feasible and deadline-1 is infeasible for the supplied route set.
