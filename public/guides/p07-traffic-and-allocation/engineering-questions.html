Engineering Questions and Socratic Vision

Discuss these after the build. Use your own traces and code to defend a decision; there are no hidden answers.

  1. Why does minimum total walking distance differ from minimum last-arrival time?
  2. What assumptions make the sum of route capacities valid?
  3. How can one shortest path prevent a better collection of longer paths?
  4. Does maximizing the number of disjoint routes by itself minimize completion time?
  5. When is a zero allocation to a valid route the right decision?
  6. What independent evidence would be needed to call the counterexample's five-turn plan globally optimal?
  7. How does the direct-tunnel case expose a common terminal-capacity misunderstanding?
