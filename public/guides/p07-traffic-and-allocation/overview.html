Overview

Build an allocation calculator for a supplied set of internally vertex-disjoint start-to-end paths. It finds the smallest completion deadline supported by those paths and constructs a valid pipeline. Then compare two supplied route sets on a colony where a greedy shortest-path reservation performs worse.

Reuse P05's distance vocabulary and P06's movement checker. This project teaches throughput, makespan and allocation without pretending that its restricted route-set calculator solves the whole colony problem. The counterexample motivates residual rerouting in P08.

Prerequisite gate

Finish these builds first: P06 · Turn Safety Simulator.

Planning estimate: 8 focused hours before review. Add 25–50% if Go or the mathematical model is new. Learner completion remains unassessed.
