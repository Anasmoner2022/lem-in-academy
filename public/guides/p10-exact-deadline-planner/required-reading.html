Required reading

Mandatory before building

  • Princeton maxflow study guide — Maximum flow/minimum cut and residual reachability. Use a failed deadline's cut as an infeasibility witness.
  • MIT 6.854 maxflow notes — Integral flows and capped augmentations. Extract ant-sized units and account for planner work.
  • Go sort package — Slice; stable ordering. Make extraction and movement output repeatable.

Recommended while building

  • Go time package — Duration; Now; Since. Measure deadline probes without writing timing diagnostics to stdout.

Reuse and readiness

Finish these builds first: P09 · Capacity Through Time.

Reading is preparation. Be ready to explain the invariant you will test before implementing it.
