Validate output and prove minimality

Join a feasibility witness with a lower-bound witness.

Before writing code

  1. Can a valid schedule still be too slow?
  2. What does a cut at T-1 prove?

Build in small passes

  1. Convert room changes to stable ID-sorted turn batches.
  2. Pass the schedule through the independently implemented P06 checker.
  3. Record either BFS lower bound at d or an exhausted T-1 cut, then explain monotonicity.

A grounded hint

The output checker proves legal motion and completion; the neighboring-deadline evidence proves minimality. Neither replaces the other.

Trace and self-check

For n=3 on a direct edge, print one ant reaching end per turn for three turns. T=2 has shared-gadget cut capacity two.

Exit gate

Every test has a valid schedule and independent lower-bound evidence, with repeated runs byte-identical.
