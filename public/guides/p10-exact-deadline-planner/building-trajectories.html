CHAPTER 7 OF 14

Extract integral ant trajectories

Turn anonymous flow into a concrete schedule without inventing moves.

Before writing code

  1. Which edge records contain final original flow?
  2. How is a gadget bounce represented in a room trajectory?

Build in small passes

  1. Create a remaining-flow copy for original arcs.
  2. Peel one stable source-sink unit at a time and decode metadata.
  3. Require n complete room trajectories of length T+1; normalize waits and retain absorbing end.

A grounded hint

A reverse residual capacity often equals the corresponding original flow, but blindly walking all residual arcs is wrong. Keep original-edge identity from P08.

Trace and self-check

Extract two ants on s-a-t at T=3. One trajectory is s,a,t,t and the other s,s,a,t, up to deterministic ID assignment.

Exit gate

Flow conservation and extracted trajectory usage agree on every original resource; no residual bookkeeping appears as a colony move.
