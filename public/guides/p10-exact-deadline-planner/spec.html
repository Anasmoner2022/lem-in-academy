Full specification

  1. Implement Plan for canonical graph data. Preflight BFS: no route returns a no-route error. Ordinary cycles and irrelevant disconnected components remain valid.
  2. Use checked d+n-1, then binary search the first feasible deadline using fresh P09/P08 state for each probe. A budget or cancellation error aborts with that distinct error; never treat it as infeasibility or fall back to a greedy answer labelled optimal.
  3. Rebuild or retain a successful model at the final T and extract exactly n integral trajectories. Validate every forward-flow unit, complete source-sink traversal and one room assignment per ant per boundary.
  4. Use stable room/edge/model ordering and always choose the first available original outgoing arc in that order for unit extraction. Ant IDs follow extraction order. Do not use map iteration or timing to break ties.
  5. Convert trajectories to moves, omit waits and sort tokens by ant ID. End is absorbing. Under the academy batch policy, a closed rotation is legal but unnecessary; it may be retained, or normalized only with a documented identity-preserving transformation and tests.
  6. Check every turn with P06 and require all ants at end. A positive empty turn in a minimum schedule would permit removing that idle turn; detect it as an invariant failure rather than printing a blank movement line.
  7. For T>d, retain an exhausted max-flow result for T-1 showing value<n and a cut with that value. For T=d, BFS provides the lower bound. Explain why feasibility at T and infeasibility at T-1 prove global minimum turns.
  8. Let U=d+n-1. With capped BFS flow and O(T(V+E)) model size, one probe costs O(nT(V+E)) and O(T(V+E)) memory. O(log n) probes give O(nU(V+E)log(n+1)) as a safe high-level bound, plus extraction/output. Storing all trajectories adds O(nT) integer cells; preflight the academy ant-time-cell budget or use a proven bounded sparse representation. Bound storage and work before allocation and include emitted token cost.
