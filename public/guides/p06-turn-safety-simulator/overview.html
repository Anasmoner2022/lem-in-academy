Overview

Build a replay checker that accepts a graph, initial ant positions and proposed movement turns. It must decide whether each turn is legal and advance state only after a complete valid batch. The checker will later challenge the planner without importing planner logic.

Reuse P04's graph and P05's reachability vocabulary. Logical concurrency here means that multiple ants move during one turn; goroutines are unnecessary. The artifact is a deterministic state transition function with adversarial tests, not a route planner.

Prerequisite gate

Finish these builds first: P05 · Queue and BFS Route Finder.

Planning estimate: 12 focused hours before review. Add 25–50% if Go or the mathematical model is new. Learner completion remains unassessed.
