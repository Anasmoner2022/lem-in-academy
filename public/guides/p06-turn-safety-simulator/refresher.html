CHAPTER 3 OF 14

Focused refresher

A turn is a batch of intents derived from the previous state. First check each ant and edge, then account for all departures and arrivals. Final occupancy of an ordinary room cannot exceed one. Start/end have unlimited occupancy; their tunnels still have capacity one.

For s-a-b-t with ants at a and b, a turn moving b→t and a→b is legal. Validate the batch independently of token order. Two opposite moves a→b and b→a use the same undirected tunnel twice and are illegal, even though final occupancy looks harmless.

type Move struct { Ant, To int } // Ant is 1-based, To is a room ID
// Derive each source from the previous positions, never from an untrusted token.

Copy state before applying a successful turn or construct next positions separately. A rejected turn must leave previous positions byte-for-byte unchanged. Finished ants remain at end. Waiting is absence from the movement list.
