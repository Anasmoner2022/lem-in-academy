Required reading

Mandatory before building

  • Princeton Algorithms: Maximum Flow — Capacities; integral flow; applications. Apply a generic capacity engine to a different domain.
  • MIT 6.854 maxflow notes — Integrality and augmenting-path invariants. Understand why one integral unit can represent one ant trajectory.
  • Official lem-in subject — Instructions: room and tunnel limits; Usage Example 2. Keep the model aligned with simultaneous pipelines.

Recommended while building

  • Go slices and maps — Slices; Maps. Keep model IDs and metadata explicit without name-dependent behavior.

Reuse and readiness

Finish these builds first: P08 · Residual Flow Workbench.

Reading is preparation. Be ready to explain the invariant you will test before implementing it.
