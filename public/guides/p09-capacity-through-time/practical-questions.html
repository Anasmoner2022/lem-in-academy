Practical Questions

Each task should take a few minutes against your finished artifact. Keep the resulting test or observation.

  1. Draw all room copies for s-a-t at T=2.
  2. Calculate the upper Vt and Et counts by hand for V=3,E=2,T=3.
  3. Remove a room split in a local experiment and exhibit the collision it permits.
  4. Replace a shared gadget by two directed arcs and exhibit the forbidden swap.
  5. Trace an early arrival through end holdovers.
  6. Add a same-side gadget normalization test.
