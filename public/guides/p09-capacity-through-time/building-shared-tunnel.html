CHAPTER 7 OF 14

Compile a shared undirected tunnel

Enforce one use total across both directions and terminal edges.

Before writing code

  1. Why are independent directed capacities insufficient?
  2. Which gadget walks represent a wait rather than a real move?

Build in small passes

  1. Create exactly one bottleneck per physical tunnel-time pair.
  2. Attach endpoint connectors with stable metadata.
  3. Omit outbound end entry connectors and document same-side normalization.

A grounded hint

Count the bottleneck crossing, not just the two endpoint choices. Unlimited start occupancy must not allow two units across s-t in one turn.

Trace and self-check

For a direct s-t graph, T=1 carries at most one and T=3 carries at most three. For a-b, opposing moves compete for the same bottleneck.

Exit gate

Tests explicitly reject double direct-edge throughput and a two-direction same-tunnel use.
