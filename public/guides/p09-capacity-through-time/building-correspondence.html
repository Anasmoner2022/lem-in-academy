CHAPTER 8 OF 14

Audit the reduction and storage bounds

Make the deadline question mathematically and operationally precise.

Before writing code

  1. Can a same-side gadget path be replaced by a wait without violating occupancy?
  2. Which arcs belong to the original DAG, and which are residual bookkeeping?

Build in small passes

  1. Write schedule→flow and flow→schedule arguments separately.
  2. Validate metadata and monotonically increasing time transitions.
  3. Estimate Vt, Et and residual arcs before allocating; return a distinct budget error.

A grounded hint

Positive final original flow is decomposed on the forward time DAG. Do not interpret reverse residual arcs as trajectories. Same-side gadget normalization preserves both endpoint occupancies.

Trace and self-check

Map the two-ant three-turn s-a-t pipeline to the network, including a start wait for ant2 and end wait for ant1.

Exit gate

A reviewer can trace every unit through each time boundary, identify the shared tunnel bottleneck and explain why the model is exact under the academy turn policy.
