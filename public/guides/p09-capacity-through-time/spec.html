Full specification

Network contract

  1. Implement Expand for a validated undirected graph, distinct terminals, positive n and nonnegative T. Return nodes, original arcs, source/sink IDs and arc metadata.
  2. Create two nodes for every (room,k), k=0..T, with capacity 1 or n as described. Use source=in(start,0) and sink=out(end,T); cap total requested flow at n.
  3. Create holdovers for every room across adjacent time layers, capacity 1 for ordinary rooms and n at terminals. An ant that arrives early stays at end; no real movement leaves end.
  4. Create one shared capacity-1 gadget per undirected tunnel per time step. Omit endpoint-entry connectors from end. Endpoint output connectors may lead to end or back to the same endpoint. Preserve physical tunnel identity and time in metadata.
  5. Never model an undirected tunnel as two independent capacity-1 movement arcs: that would allow a forbidden opposing swap. Never use n as a direct start-end tunnel's capacity.
  6. Node IDs and original arc insertion order are deterministic from P04's canonical room and edge ordering. Metadata distinguishes room, wait, connector and bottleneck arcs without trying to infer meaning from numeric IDs later.
  7. Before allocation, calculate checked upper counts: Vt=2(T+1)V+2TE and Et≤(2T+1)V+5TE original arcs. Residual storage doubles Et. Reject a configured budget breach as resource exhaustion, not malformed colony or infeasible deadline.

Correspondence argument

A legal schedule gives integral flow by following room splits, waits and each moved tunnel gadget; continue early arrivals through end holdovers. Capacities follow from batch room occupancy and shared tunnel use. Conversely, integral value-n flow decomposes into n source-sink walks in the original time DAG. Read each layer transition as a move or wait; normalize same-side gadget uses into waits. Room splits and bottlenecks then enforce all P06 constraints. No flow means fewer than n ants can meet that deadline. Absorbing end is without loss: keeping an arrived ant there frees rather than consumes nonterminal resources.
