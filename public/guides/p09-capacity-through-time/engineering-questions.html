Engineering Questions and Socratic Vision

Discuss these after the build. Use your own traces and code to defend a decision; there are no hidden answers.

  1. Why does static max flow alone fail to answer the minimum completion-turn question?
  2. Which precise rule requires vertex splitting rather than edge capacities alone?
  3. What would two independent directed tunnel arcs allow that upstream forbids?
  4. Why is a same-side gadget traversal harmless only when normalized correctly?
  5. How can terminal holdover capacity and terminal tunnel capacity be different?
  6. Why does a failed model allocation provide no evidence that a deadline is infeasible?
  7. What would change if ants could traverse two tunnels during one turn?
