CHAPTER 3 OF 14

Focused refresher

A static max-flow graph measures throughput, not a completion deadline. Time expansion gives each room a copy at every boundary k=0..T. A unit flow travels from start at time 0 to end at time T.

Split each room-time copy into in and out nodes. The in→out arc has capacity 1 for an ordinary room and n for start/end. A holdover out(v,k)→in(v,k+1) represents waiting and uses the same boundary occupancy limits.

For each physical tunnel {u,v} and turn k→k+1, create two gadget nodes ein/eout with one capacity-1 bottleneck ein→eout. Connect out(u,k) and out(v,k) into ein; connect eout to in(u,k+1) and in(v,k+1). All gadget connector capacities are 1. Both directions share the bottleneck, so an opposing swap cannot consume the tunnel twice.

The gadget also permits entering and leaving on the same side. Interpret that as waiting, not a self-tunnel move, and normalize it to the holdover during extraction. It cannot create extra movement capacity. Do not add gadget entry connectors from end: end is absorbing, while its holdovers retain arrivals through T.
