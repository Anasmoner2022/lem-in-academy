CHAPTER 6 OF 14

Compile room-time capacity

Represent occupancy at boundaries rather than limiting only incoming edges.

Before writing code

  1. At which times must an ordinary room have capacity one?
  2. Why do early arrivals need end holdovers?

Build in small passes

  1. Assign stable in/out IDs for every room and layer.
  2. Add split arcs and holdovers with terminal exceptions.
  3. Print the node/arc inventory for a two-room one-turn model.

A grounded hint

Two ants entering an ordinary room through different tunnels can bypass per-edge limits. Only the shared room split prevents both occupying that room at a boundary.

Trace and self-check

For s-a-t and T=2, mark a at times 0,1,2. One ant can occupy a at time 1; the second cannot also occupy it then.

Exit gate

A flow test cannot deliver two ants through s-a-t by T=2, but can by T=3 after the tunnel gadgets are added.
